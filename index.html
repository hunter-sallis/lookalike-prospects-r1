Key findings

7 findings · select one to read it
A gap here means one of three things: no MLC work carries the hit, a work is registered but part of it is unclaimed, or the hit’s ISRC is attached to someone else’s song. Together these hits carry 33,100,000 measured Spotify plays. Registration status is never payment status — nothing here says what any artist has or has not collected.
An exact ISRC search and an artist + recording-title search both came back empty for Prophet the Artist (“Irregular”, 5,700,000 plays), Cesar Smoove (“Dictadura”, 4,100,000 plays), Shawn Scrilla (“Sing”, 3,300,000 plays), Najjee (“Cherry Pie”, 2,900,000 plays), SANTANA ALI (“Boy Meets World”, 2,500,000 plays), Ace Volar (“Violence”, 2,400,000 plays). Four of them — Prophet the Artist, Shawn Scrilla, Najjee and SANTANA ALI — do have other works at The MLC; the hit is the one that is missing. That is absence in the public search, not proof of absence — the first question for each artist is whether they have a society, an IPI number, and registrations under their legal name.
A title collision matches a recording to an unrelated composition with a similar title, so usage on the recording is credited to that other work. Each one needs a dispute of the wrong link and a registration of the artist’s own work.
  • Brickboydior — ISRC is attached to 'RHYTHM OF LIFE' by Thomas Austin Hinds — a different composition. The artist's actual song has no work; the collision routes the artist's mechanicals to a stranger (same pattern as TJ Dizzle's 13 overclaims). Dispute + register lane. Hit label = Alamo.
A work exists and the recording is attached, but the claimed shares stop short of 100%. In most of these, a writer on the work has no IPI number and no publisher — the likely open seat.
  • Jaywall — “HIM” · 50% claimed. ISRC linked. Only the co-writer's side is claimed; Jaylen Wallace (≈ Jaywall) has no IPI and no publisher on the work — the artist's 50% is the open half. 5.7M-stream song.
  • 360Zayy — “Insomnia” · 50% claimed. ISRC linked. Producer half (BeatStars) claimed; Isaiah Daniels (≈ 360Zayy) has no IPI and no publisher — the artist's 50% is open. TuneCore-distributed.
  • Lil Spooki — ISRC matched to TWO works — a self-registration and a Songtrust registration of the same song. Spooki's side is claimed on both; Zimmerie's is open on work 2. Conflict/merge lane, not a pitch gap.
FNF Chop (“Walk Down”, 9,800,000 plays), Ja'king the Divine (“Shawn and Marlon's Gem$”, 7,000,000 plays), KT6900 (“Dark Knights”, 2,400,000 plays), 74 Youngan (“The Street Escape”, 2,200,000 plays). Each has a work at 100% known shares with the recording attached.
This pass covers The MLC only. BMI, ASCAP and GMR performance registrations were not searched, and the master side — SoundExchange, where a producer’s share is paid through a Letter of Direction — is a separate review.

What happens next

  1. Build full gap reports on the Florida pair

    Jaywall (South Florida) and Ace Volar (Tallahassee) are the strongest round-1 leads: Jaywall's own half of “HIM” is open, and Ace Volar's hit has no work at all. Both get the full catalog treatment next.

  2. Package the Brickboydior title collision

    The “Life Of Brick” ISRC sits on an unrelated work (“RHYTHM OF LIFE”). The dispute of that link and a fresh registration of the real song go together — but Alamo is on the hit, so confirm who administers it before any outreach.

  3. Confirm identity on the no-work set

    For Ace Volar, Cesar Smoove, Prophet the Artist, Shawn Scrilla, Najjee and SANTANA ALI, the first conversation is identity: legal name, society, IPI number, and any existing registrations. Nothing is filed without documentation.

  4. Drop the claimed, route the duplicate

    FNF Chop, Ja'king the Divine, KT6900 and 74 Youngan are fully claimed. Lil Spooki's song is registered twice — a merge, not a pitch.

Basis and limits. Internal prospect research prepared from public data: Spotify artist pages, the Deezer catalog and The MLC public work search (exact ISRC first, artist + recording title for the misses, then each work’s detail record). “No MLC work found” is absence in the public search, not proof of absence. A registration is a set of payment instructions, never payment itself — nothing here is a statement about money paid or owed. BMI, ASCAP, GMR and SoundExchange were not searched.
